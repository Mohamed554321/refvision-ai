# RefVision AI — Privacy, Security, & Fair Play

## 1. Synthetic Data & Fictional Identities

In strict adherence to the competition guidelines:
- All player identities, team rosters, and match personnel are entirely fictional (e.g., *Apex Vanguard FC* vs *Crown Meridian FC*, striker *Marcus Vance*, referee *Marcus Sterling*).
- No actual Premier League players, match officials, or real individuals are simulated, ranked, or penalized by the platform.
- The platform does not ingest unverified private camera footage or unauthorized video feeds.

---

## 2. Fair Play & Responsible Gamification

RefVision AI's **Fan Arena** prediction experience is designed purely for community engagement and fan education:
- **Zero Financial Wagering:** There is no real-money betting, gambling mechanics, or financial stakes.
- **Non-Monetary Rewards:** Achievements unlock digital certificates, fan badges, and community leaderboard ranks.
- **Strict Anti-Cheating & Immutability:**
  * Submissions lock automatically when the countdown deadline expires.
  * Duplicate entries are rejected.
  * Prediction outcomes are resolved by code against subsequent verified match events. No language model can invent or change game outcomes.

---

## 3. Data Protection

- The platform does not store sensitive personal information or third-party cookies.
- Demo profiles (`Alex_PremierFan`) are persisted locally via browser localStorage.
- API endpoints operate locally without transmitting user data to third-party ad networks or brokers.
